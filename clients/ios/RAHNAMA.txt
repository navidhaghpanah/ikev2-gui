   (AirDrop / Telegram / email / Files).
2) Ruye file tap kon. Payam: Profile Downloaded.

   (ya Settings > General > VPN & Device Management)

5) Settings > VPN > IKEv2 > Connect

   Server / Remote ID: __DOMAIN__
